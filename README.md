# BusinessLedger

A lightweight business management prototype for a small shop offering product sales and repair services.

## Live demo
[Open BusinessLedger](https://nnekaanadu-art.github.io/businessledger-lite/)

## Overview
BusinessLedger helps a single business owner record individual transactions, reconcile daily cash, and understand profitability without the complexity of a full accounting system.

## Features
- **Dashboard:** Review revenue, costs, estimated profit, product sales versus repairs, and cash versus card payments.
- **Ledger:** Record, edit, and review individual sales, repair income, and business expenses.
- **Daily Close:** Compare expected cash with the amount counted and save closing records.
- **Data portability:** Export transactions to CSV and back up or restore records using JSON.
- **Responsive interface:** Designed for desktop and mobile browsers.

## How to use
Open the live demo, or download `index.html` and open it in a browser. Load sample data to explore the dashboard, add transactions in Ledger, and use Daily Close to reconcile cash.

## Important notes
This is an interactive **portfolio prototype**, not production accounting software. Records are stored locally in your browser; they are not synchronized between devices. Avoid entering real sensitive business or customer information. Use the JSON backup feature to retain test data.

## Product rationale
The prototype responds to a small business owner's need to distinguish product sales from repair income, track cash and card payments, record expenses, and roll daily results into useful financial summaries. The application prioritizes simple daily workflows over advanced accounting features.
